# CRAI Demo Guide

## Recommended Demo Flow

### 1. Start with the problem

Explain that crop assessment can involve visual symptoms plus environmental and field conditions.

### 2. Show the Overview

Demonstrate the dashboard as the central system view.

Highlight:

- Current crop intelligence
- System status
- Recent observations
- Available field evidence

### 3. Demonstrate Observe

Provide a crop image and show the image-analysis workflow.

Explain that the image is one source of evidence rather than the entire decision.

### 4. Demonstrate Sensors

Show field/environmental observations and device status.

Explain how sensor evidence can provide additional context.

### 5. Demonstrate Intelligence

Move to the Intelligence view and explain how the platform organizes available evidence into a risk-oriented interpretation.

### 6. Demonstrate Advisory

Show how the AI-assisted layer translates technical results into understandable guidance.

### 7. Show History / Reports

Finish by showing that observations and analysis can be reviewed rather than disappearing after a single interaction.

## Judge-Friendly Explanation

A concise explanation:

> "CRAI is an evidence-aware crop-risk intelligence platform. It combines crop-image analysis with field observations, processes the available evidence, produces a structured risk view, and converts that intelligence into understandable advisory information through a unified dashboard."

## Important Presentation Note

Do not reveal private model weights, unpublished thresholds, internal prompts or proprietary decision rules during a public repository/demo walkthrough unless they are intentionally approved for disclosure.
